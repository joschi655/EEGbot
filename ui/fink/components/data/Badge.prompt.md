A compliance status pill. Tone communicates the domain state.

```jsx
<Badge tone="compliant" dot>Konform</Badge>
<Badge tone="pending" dot>Frist in 6 Tagen</Badge>
<Badge tone="overdue" dot>Überfällig</Badge>
<Badge tone="neutral">Entwurf</Badge>
```

Tones: `compliant` · `pending` · `overdue` · `neutral` · `info` · `solid`. Props: `dot`, `size` (`sm`/`lg`), `icon`.
